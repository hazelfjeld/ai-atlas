Continuous batching

Dynamically adding and removing generation requests between inference steps rather than waiting for an entire batch to finish.

Original reference page · detailed expansion pending. Original definitions, examples, notes and citations are preserved below. New examples are illustrative, not benchmark results.

Original caveat
