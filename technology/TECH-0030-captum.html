Captum

PyTorch model-interpretability library.

Reference link only — not reviewed this edition. This profile preserves the prepared catalog’s original descriptions. Advantages depend on the workload; comparisons are editorial, not benchmark results.

Where it has an edge

Common attribution implementations reduce reimplementation work for gradients, perturbations and influence-style analysis.

Best-fit problems

Explaining supported PyTorch models and testing attribution hypotheses.

Tradeoffs & cautions

Attribution is method-dependent and not necessarily a faithful causal account.

What to test before choosing

Use controlled baselines and counterexamples; test false positives, robustness under shift and validity of conclusions.
