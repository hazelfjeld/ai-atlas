Hugging Face PEFT

Parameter-efficient model adaptation methods.

Core capability checked in primary documentation. This profile preserves the prepared catalog’s original descriptions. Advantages depend on the workload; comparisons are editorial, not benchmark results.

Where it has an edge

Training small adapter parameters lowers optimizer and gradient storage versus full fine-tuning.

Best-fit problems

LoRA-style adaptation when full-model training is too expensive.

Tradeoffs & cautions

Base weights and activations still consume memory; adapters do not guarantee equivalent quality.

What to test before choosing

Compare held-out quality, forgetting, peak memory and total training cost against the unadapted/full-precision baseline.
