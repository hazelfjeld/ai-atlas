Where it has an edge

A consistent reset/step contract makes algorithms and environments interchangeable.

Best-fit problems

Designing and evaluating RL environments and agents.

Tradeoffs & cautions

An API standard does not make environment rewards meaningful or realistic.

What to test before choosing

Validate rewards and environment termination; run multiple seeds and check for reward exploitation and sim-to-real gaps.
