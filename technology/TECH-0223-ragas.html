Ragas

Evaluation toolkit for retrieval and LLM pipelines.

Official page located/opened — partial review. This profile preserves the prepared catalog’s original descriptions. Advantages depend on the workload; comparisons are editorial, not benchmark results.

Where it has an edge

Separate retrieval and response metrics help diagnose RAG failures beyond answer-only scoring.

Best-fit problems

Evaluating document retrieval, grounding and response usefulness.

Tradeoffs & cautions

Reference-free metrics are not ground truth; judge calibration and representative samples matter.

What to test before choosing

Calibrate scorers on human-labeled cases; fix versions, sampling settings and confidence intervals before comparing.
