TECHNOLOGY PROFILE · TECH-0220

pyvene

Library for interventions on model representations.

Reference link only — not reviewed this edition. This profile preserves the prepared catalog’s original descriptions. Advantages depend on the workload; comparisons are editorial, not benchmark results.

Where it has an edge

Structured intervention specifications make causal representation experiments easier to reproduce.

Best-fit problems

Activation edits and causal abstraction studies.

Tradeoffs & cautions

Interventions can be off-distribution and do not automatically imply causal explanation of real inputs.

What to test before choosing

Use controlled baselines and counterexamples; test false positives, robustness under shift and validity of conclusions.
