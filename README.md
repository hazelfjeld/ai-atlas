# Atlas — AI technology & safety observatory

Atlas combines a searchable AI technology catalog, concept dictionary, problem playbooks, research library, and evidence-labeled safety observatory.

## Publication status

The repository has been created. The prepared app is being imported and validated; this README does **not** yet establish that the site is live.

The planned entry point is `index.html` on `main`:

https://raw.githack.com/hazelfjeld/ai-atlas/main/index.html

The prepared catalog contains 300 technology profiles, 1,345 concepts, 20 playbooks, 30 paper records, and 10 safety report records. It is a curated starter collection, not every AI safety paper or incident. Automatic research collection is not active.

No credentials or private notebook contents belong in this public repository.
